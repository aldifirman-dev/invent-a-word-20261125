This entry coins **retrowince**, a single-word noun naming the sudden, involuntary physical flinch triggered during an ordinary quiet moment—such as showering or trying to fall asleep—when an uninvited memory of an old embarrassment resurfaces. English has words for moral guilt (*remorse*) and broad discomfort (*embarrassment*), yet lacks a concise term for that sharp, retroactive cringe that ambushes the mind years after the fact.
